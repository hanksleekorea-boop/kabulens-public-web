Stock Scanner · Advertising disclosure

Advertising cannot purchase a research outcome.

Current status: live third-party advertising and direct campaigns are not active. The public service contains no live marketplace, bidder, publisher, or campaign identifier and makes no advertising request.

Where advertising may appear

If all account, legal, privacy, security, and supply-chain checks are completed, clearly labeled advertising may appear in no more than two reserved areas on the English educational guide. The scanner, rankings, reports, saved research, policy pages, support, print, and offline mode remain ad-free.

Editorial independence

  • An advertiser cannot buy inclusion, a score, a rank, a favorable explanation, or removal of contrary evidence.
  • Advertising teams and demand partners cannot change methodology weights, synthetic results, or research conclusions.
  • A commercial relationship must be disclosed and separated from educational content.

Privacy boundary

  • Watchlists, saved scans, research notes, imports, symbols, company searches, and account identity cannot be used to build advertising audiences.
  • Stock Scanner does not create an advertising user identifier. Marketplace reporting is limited to aggregate delivery, revenue, failure, and user-experience measures.
  • Applicable certified consent, rejection, withdrawal, GPC, and age signals must be resolved before any third-party auction.

Campaign eligibility

A live direct campaign requires a verified advertiser and billing contact, signed insertion order, approved creative and landing page, delivery and frequency limits, tax and invoicing review, cancellation and make-good terms, and independent editorial approval. Misleading financial claims, guaranteed returns, unlicensed brokerage, gambling, high-risk lending, malware, forced redirects, and deceptive controls are prohibited.

Interest before activation

The service is not currently accepting a live campaign. For a non-sensitive preliminary question, use the public support page. Do not post contracts, payment information, personal information, credentials, or unpublished campaign material in a public issue.
