Stock Scanner · Privacy choices

Advertising and privacy choices

Live advertising is currently disabled. No Stock Scanner publisher identifier or ad-unit identifier is configured, so this page cannot activate third-party advertising.

When advertising is activated

A certified consent platform will present the choices required for your region. Rejecting advertising consent or withdrawing it later will keep the educational guide available and will not disable the synthetic scanner.

Global strict default

  • No advertising request before an accepted certified signal when one is required.
  • No advertising in scanner controls, results, policy pages, support, print, or offline mode.
  • No advertising audience derived from your watchlist, research notes, or imported files.
  • No deceptive financial, gambling, high-risk lending, or guaranteed-return promotions.

Clear local advertising preferences

This removes any Stock Scanner advertising preference saved in this browser. It does not delete scanner research data and cannot delete records held independently by a browser or hosting provider.

Other controls

Your browser may provide cookie blocking, site-data deletion, Global Privacy Control, tracking prevention, or private browsing. The scanner’s “My data” area separately controls locally saved research data.
